Welcome to **ChessLens**, the ultimate post-game analysis tool for the true chess-o-holic. Whether you're hanging queens in time trouble, grinding the endgame like Magnus, or just trying to figure out why your "brilliant sacrifice" was actually a +M3 for your opponent, ChessLens has you covered. 

We connect directly to your favorite arenas (Lichess and Chess.com), pull your PGNs, and run them through the digital wringer to help you gain that sweet, sweet Elo.

---

## 🌟 The Repertoire (Features)

*   **The Opening Setup:** Seamlessly fetch games from both **Chess.com** and **Lichess.org** using just a username. No manual PGN copying required!
*   **Tactical Vision:** Deep engine analysis to evaluate your moves. We'll highlight your *Brilliant (!)* moves, forgive your *Inaccuracies (?)*, and ruthlessly point out your *Blunders (??)*.
*   **Time-Scramble Analytics:** See how you perform under pressure in Bullet, Blitz, and Rapid time controls.
*   **Opening Book Explorer:** Identify which variations of the Sicilian are winning you rating points, and which ones are sending you back to the lobby.
